# Campus Rush

A 2D top-down arcade game made in Godot 4 (GDScript). Find your way from home to college through the city.

## The route
Home -> turn LEFT at the junction -> turn RIGHT at the Cake Shop -> cross the HIGHWAY (wait for the signal)
-> go STRAIGHT past DMart -> turn RIGHT at Burger King -> COLLEGE.
Wrong turns lead to dead ends (Temple, Park, Library).

## Controls
- Move: WASD or Arrow keys
- Restart / next level: R or Enter

## Features
- Scrolling city map with a minimap, step-by-step directions and a distance-to-college counter
- 4-lane highway with a working traffic signal (cars stop on red, WALK board for pedestrians)
- People who walk between turning-point signs (bumping one costs 2 seconds)
- Manholes, trees, bushes and flower pots
- Sound effects: car horns, tyre screech on close calls, crash, signal ding and pedestrian beeps, bump, dead end, win jingle
- Pixel-art sprites in `assets/` (replace any PNG with your own, same file name)

## Run
1. Install Godot 4.2+
2. Open `project.godot` in Godot
3. Press F5
